## What Is a Serialized Arc?

A serialized arc is a sequence of related clusters designed to be experienced over multiple dates, building a narrative or thematic progression.

**Example structures:**
- **The Collector's Journey:** 3 dates, each revealing a different layer of obscure art/history
- **Taste Map:** 4 dates exploring a single cuisine through increasingly niche lenses
- **Secret City:** 5 dates uncovering hidden layers of NYC (architecture, infrastructure, subcultures)
