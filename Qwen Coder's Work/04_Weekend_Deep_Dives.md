## What Is a Weekend Deep Dive?

A deep dive is a longer-form cluster designed for weekends or days with more time — typically an afternoon-to-evening arc or full-day exploration.

**Key differences from Evening clusters:**
- More legs (4–6 vs. 2–4)
- Includes daytime-specific activities (markets, workshops, outdoor elements)
- Allows for farther geographic range
- Can include meal + snack + drink pacing
