# Date Night Planning Reference

## Purpose

This is the compact planning packet for a later LLM to use after the girlfriend reacts to a menu of date options. It consolidates the available repository context into one stripped-down reference. No venue research has been regenerated and no new venues have been added.

## Source Status

The repository currently contains no source date-night research files beyond repository metadata, so there are no existing venue lists, modular clusters, weekend deep dives, serial arcs, or verification labels to dedupe. The preserved planning material below comes from the provided prompt context only.

## People and Logistics

- Couple: two people in their 30s, cultured, food-curious, and interested in music, arts, oddities, history, museums, whimsy, and hidden corners of the city.
- Home bases:
  - Him: East 64th Street between 2nd Avenue and 1st Avenue, Upper East Side.
  - Her: West 98th Street and Amsterdam Avenue, Upper West Side.
- Work bases:
  - Her work: West 55th Street near 9th/10th Avenue; typically leaves around 5:30 PM.
  - His work: Battery Park / Staten Island Ferry area; can leave early.
- Weeknight pacing:
  - Build around an early-ish meal because she gets hungry early.
  - Avoid late nights; plans should feel complete without dragging on.
  - Favor efficient geography: near her work, near one of the home bases, or in a neighborhood close enough that transit does not dominate the date.

## Planning Vocabulary

- **Cluster**: a complete date plan with multiple coordinated stops.
- **Leg types**:
  - **Fuel**: meal, drink, snack, dessert, or culinary anchor.
  - **Spark**: primary activity, exhibit, performance-adjacent experience, class, object of attention, or discovery moment.
  - **Browse**: low-pressure shop/gallery/bookstore/market browsing.
  - **Lesson**: structured learning, tasting, making, guided history, or skill-based component.
  - **Secret Sauce**: intimate, strange, unusually specific, romantic, sensory, or IYKYK detail that makes the plan feel chosen rather than generic.
  - **Detour**: optional add-on that should be geographically logical and easy to skip.
- **Reach**: an orthogonal, higher-surprise suggestion that still respects the couple's constraints.
- **Duration archetypes**:
  - **Micromoment**: very short date fragment or add-on.
  - **Evening**: weeknight-compatible early meal plus one meaningful activity.
  - **Afternoon**: weekend or flexible-day plan with more wandering room.
  - **Full Day**: substantial multi-part city day.
  - **Day Trip**: outside ordinary city radius.
  - **Serialized**: repeatable arc across multiple dates.

## Taste Profile to Preserve

- Desired texture: wholesome and thoughtful with a romantic/sensual undercurrent.
- Best-fit themes:
  - Oddities and hidden gems.
  - IYKYK or under-the-radar places.
  - Avant-garde or experimental culture, but not alienating.
  - Obscure history and niche museums/collections.
  - Niche culinary experiences.
  - Whimsy held in productive tension with seriousness.
  - Things to do, inspect, taste, learn, handle, decode, compare, or look at.
- Plans should feel intentional, cultured, and slightly secret, not like generic NYC date templates.

## Hard Constraints

- No traditional theatrical productions; she works in theater.
- No loud music venues.
- No basic park or waterfront strolls unless materially elevated by a specific object, route, historic frame, rare view, guided structure, or other non-generic hook.
- No meal-only plans. Every cluster needs at least one activity or thing to do/look at in addition to food or drink.
- Avoid plans that run too late or require delaying dinner too long.

- Shukette + High Line + Chelsea galleries.

## Geographic Strategy

When building options later, prefer one of these logics:

1. **Post-work west-side ease**: start near West 55th and 9th/10th after her 5:30 PM departure, with early Fuel nearby and a second stop that does not require a long transfer.
2. **Near him / Upper East Side-adjacent**: cluster around East 64th between 2nd and 1st, or nearby neighborhoods, if he is hosting, meeting first, or wants a polished but easy night.
3. **Near her / Upper West Side-adjacent without banned zone**: avoid UWS 80s–100s clusters, but adjacent geography can work if it does not feel like replaying the banned category.
4. **Equidistant or transit-clean middle**: choose neighborhoods that are natural from both Battery Park/SI Ferry and West 55th after work, especially if the route makes the night feel fluid.
5. **Weekend expansion**: if not a weeknight, allow Afternoon, Full Day, Day Trip, or Serialized structures, but retain the couple's appetite for specificity and avoid generic wandering.

## Cluster-Building Rules for Later LLM Use

- Start by identifying her reaction: what she liked, disliked, or hesitated over.
- Convert that reaction into 2–4 clusters, not a giant list.
- Each cluster should include:
  - A concise title.
  - Neighborhood / geography logic.
  - Duration archetype.
  - Ordered legs using the planning vocabulary.
  - A clear early Fuel component for weeknights.
  - At least one Spark, Browse, Lesson, Secret Sauce, or Detour beyond the meal.
  - Why it fits her stated reaction and the couple's taste profile.
  - Any verification labels already present in source material, if future source files provide them: **Standing**, **Recurring**, **Limited-Run**, or **[UNVERIFIED]**.
- Keep stop order natural: do not zigzag; do not add transit friction for novelty's sake.
- Prefer density over sprawl. A two-stop plan with a strong Secret Sauce is better than a four-stop plan that feels logistically thin.
- For weeknights, default to early dinner or substantial snack first, then the activity, unless the activity has a fixed early time and food is immediately before/after.
- Make every plan legible as a date: thoughtful, intimate, and specific.

## What Not to Do Later

- Do not add generic filler such as “grab drinks nearby” unless the place or reason is specific.
- Do not propose theater, loud concerts, standard museum blockbuster defaults, generic gallery wandering, undifferentiated park walks, or meal-only itineraries.
- Do not revive banned anchors by swapping in near-identical substitutes.
- Do not overbuild. The goal is a few viable versions of a night, not exhaustive research.
